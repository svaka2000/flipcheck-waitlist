# Creator / reseller-influencer outreach

Reseller creators already have your exact audience. One creator doing a "I tried this app"
video can out-perform weeks of your own posting. Target **micro creators (10k–150k)** —
they reply, they're affordable (often free for early access + a kickback), and their
audiences are more engaged than mega-accounts.

## How to find them (do this, don't buy a list)

Search these on **TikTok, YouTube, and Instagram** and save every creator who posts
regularly with real engagement:

- `thrift flip`, `thrift haul`, `reselling`, `ebay reseller`, `whatnot reseller`
- `poshmark tips`, `depop seller`, `garage sale flip`, `estate sale haul`
- `sourcing for resale`, `flea market finds`, `goodwill bins`

Build a simple sheet: **Handle · Platform · Followers · Avg views · Email/DM · Status.**
Aim for a list of 50; DM 10/day.

## Tiered offer

- **Micro (10–50k):** free lifetime Pro + a unique referral/affiliate link + "founding
  creator" credit in-app. Usually enough.
- **Mid (50–150k):** the above + a small flat fee or per-signup bounty.
- **Affiliate for everyone:** their `?ref=` link already tracks signups — give top
  referrers a public shout + extra Pro. (Your product already powers this.)

## DM template (warm, specific, short)

> Hey [name] — I love your [specific recent video, e.g. "the $3 Pyrex flip"]. I built
> **FlipCheck**: point your camera at a thrift item and it gives you a resale range +
> Buy/Maybe/Skip instantly. It's launching on iOS and I'd love to give you free early
> access (and a creator referral link) to try on your next sourcing run — no strings. If
> it's useful and you feel like showing it, amazing; if not, no worries at all. Want me
> to send you in?

## Follow-up (if no reply in 4–5 days)

> Hey [name], no worries if it's not your thing — just wanted to make sure this didn't get
> buried. Early access + a creator link is yours if you want to kick the tires. 🙏

## What to give them so it's easy to post

- Early TestFlight access (once the build is live — see app's HANDOFF/G4 gate).
- Their unique `?ref=` link (jumps their followers up the line = a reason to share).
- The clip kit: `public/og.png`, screenshots, and the 22s demo video.
- 2–3 ready hooks from `tiktok-reels.md` they can riff on.

## The compounding play

Every creator's audience that signs up gets *their own* referral link → the loop keeps
multiplying past the original video. That's why product-side referrals + creator seeding
together beat either alone.
