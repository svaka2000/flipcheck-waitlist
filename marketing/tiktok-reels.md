# Short-form video — the #1 growth lever

Thrift-flip content is one of the most viral niches on TikTok/Reels/Shorts. **One clip,
posted to all three.** Goal: 1 video/day for 2 weeks, then keep the winners going.

The format that wins: **POV in the store → scan → reveal the number → reaction.** People
stay for the "what's it worth" reveal (it's the same dopamine as those appraisal shows).

## The 6 hooks that work (first 1.5 seconds is everything)

1. **"I scanned everything at Goodwill so you don't have to."** (montage of scans + verdicts)
2. **"This $4 [item] is worth HOW much??"** (freeze on the price reveal)
3. **"POV: you finally know what's worth flipping before you buy it."**
4. **"Rating thrift store finds by resale value (the app does it instantly)."**
5. **"Stop overpaying at thrift stores. Watch this."**
6. **"3 things I almost walked past that the app said BUY."**

## Script template (15–30s)

```
[0:00] HOOK on screen text + you talking: "I scanned everything at Goodwill..."
[0:02] Quick cuts: phone camera over 3–4 items
[0:06] Tap → verdict pops: "$48–72 — BUY ↑"  (let it breathe 1 sec, big reaction)
[0:12] One more: "SKIP ↓" on a dud — "saved me $20"
[0:18] Payoff: "This isn't out yet — link in bio for early access, first users get it free."
[0:22] End card: FlipCheck logo + "Know what it's worth before you buy it."
```

## Posting mechanics

- **Link in bio** → use a 1-link page or just the waitlist URL. Put the URL **in the first
  pinned comment too** ("link's in my bio 👆").
- Post natively to each platform (don't post a TikTok with the watermark to Reels — the
  algo suppresses it). Re-export clean or use CapCut without watermark.
- Best times: 11am–1pm and 7–10pm local. Post daily; the algorithm rewards consistency.
- **Reply to every comment with a video reply** in the first hour — massive reach boost.
- When one hits, **immediately make 3 variations** of that exact hook.
