## Build-in-public cadence (1–2/week)

These quietly attract the indie-hacker + reseller crowd and keep the waitlist growing:

- **Milestones:** "Just crossed 500 on the FlipCheck waitlist 🎉 here's what's working…"
- **Teardowns:** "Scanned 20 random thrift items to stress-test the verdict — results:"
- **Lessons:** "What I learned pricing resale with AI vision (the hard parts)."
- **Polls:** "Resellers: what kills you most in the aisle? ① pricing speed ② fees ③ fakes"
- **Asks:** "What category should FlipCheck nail first — sneakers, vintage tees, or electronics?"

## Who to tag / reply to

- Reply (with value, not spam) to reseller accounts posting hauls/"what's it worth."
- Indie founders who boost build-in-public: search "#buildinpublic waitlist".
- Quote-tweet thrift/flip viral posts with "this is exactly why I built X" + clip.
